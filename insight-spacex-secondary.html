Run Capital Partners invested in SpaceX ahead of its public market debut

A secondary market transaction in one of the world’s leading aerospace and space technology companies.

Run Capital Partners Private Markets

In February 2026, Run Capital Partners, through one of the funds within its investment ecosystem, participated in a secondary market transaction involving SpaceX, one of the world’s leading aerospace and space technology companies.

At the time of the transaction, SpaceX was valued at approximately $980 billion. While the valuation appeared demanding to many market participants, the investment reflected Run Capital Partners’ conviction in the company’s technological leadership, competitive positioning, and long-term growth potential.

In June 2026, SpaceX completed its public market debut at an IPO valuation of approximately $1.77 trillion. The company now trades at a market capitalization of approximately $1.9 trillion, nearly double the valuation at the time of Run Capital Partners’ investment.

The transaction reflects Run Capital Partners’ investment strategy: identifying industry-leading private companies, accessing attractive entry conditions across private and secondary markets, and maintaining a long-term approach to value creation.
