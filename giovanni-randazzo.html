Giovanni Randazzo

VP Institutional Clients

LinkedIn Profile

Giovanni serves as Vice President of Run Capital Partners and Director of RRCAPITAL S.A. (Luxembourg), leading the Group's institutional coordination across alternative-investment funds and securitization vehicles. With more than 25 years of experience across Italy, Luxembourg, London and Brussels, he has held senior roles in alternative-fund and private-equity managers, regulated SGRs and industrial groups — and previously served as Head of the Technical Secretariat to the Minister at the Italian Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Forestry Policies.
