Antonio Franchino

Director, Run Asset Management

Antonio leads Run Asset Management, the group’s Luxembourg AIFM. A lawyer by training, he brings more than 25 years in compliance, anti-money laundering and legal affairs across Italian banking and insurance groups, and since 2019 has sat on the boards of Luxembourg RAIF funds, overseeing internal compliance and the relationship with management companies, auditors and depositary banks.
